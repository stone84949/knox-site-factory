Plain answers

Before the first call

Who owns the website?

The client owns the domain, approved content, analytics, and business accounts. The agreement will state what transfers at launch and what remains licensed, such as third-party media.

Does the price include ongoing SEO?

No ranking campaign is bundled into the launch. The site includes a sound technical foundation: crawlable pages, useful titles and descriptions, structured business information, a sitemap, and indexability checks.

What does the care plan cover?

Hosting, backups, monitoring, security updates, up to 30 minutes of small edits each month, and a quarterly website-health review. New pages, campaigns, photography, and large revisions are separate work.

How long does a build take?

The schedule is set after the fit call, based on how ready your services, photos, and service-area details are. Nothing goes live until you approve the facts.

Are the three demos real businesses?

No. They are clearly labeled concept demos. They contain no real testimonials, ratings, licenses, customer counts, or performance results.
