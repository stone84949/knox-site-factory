Fair questions from business owners.

"I get most of my work from referrals."

That's the best kind of work. Most referrals still look you up before they call, to check they've got the right company and find the number. The site makes that moment quick and makes calling easy.

"I don't have time to write a website."

You don't write it. We have one short call about your services, your area and what customers ask. Josh drafts the words; you correct the facts and approve.

"My current site is fine."

It might be. The free 15-minute site review is Josh checking it on a phone the way a customer would. If it's not worth replacing, he'll tell you.

"Someone else controls my domain."

That's common. We'll sort out who controls it before any work starts, so you're not paying for a site you can't own.

"Will it pay for itself?"

Nobody can honestly promise that, including us. But you can do the math for your own business.
