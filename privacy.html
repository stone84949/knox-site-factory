Privacy

Updated October 27, 2026. This notice describes the current Knox Site Factory website and contact options.

When you contact Josh

You can contact Josh Stone by email at hello@knoxsitefactory.com or by calling or texting 865-551-9084. Your message may include your name, business name, contact details, website and project information. We use that information to answer you and, if you become a client, manage the project and its business records.

The review buttons open your email app. They do not send a message from this website or subscribe you to marketing. Email and telephone providers process the communications you send through them.

Browser storage and website forms

On pages that load our source-label script, a label such as the source of a link can be stored in your browser’s session storage for the visit. This helps retain the source label as you move between pages. It is not a mailing-list signup. The email-app review path does not automatically send that label to a lead spreadsheet.

The homepage and review page do not currently submit a web form. Forms in fictional sample sites and the staging intake check fields in the browser and do not send their contents.

Analytics

We do not currently run an active website analytics beacon. The proposed Cloudflare analytics integration is disabled. We will update this notice if the way we collect website data changes.

The hosting provider processes technical requests needed to serve website pages. Its handling of service logs is separate from the optional analytics beacon described above.

Payments

Payment links take you to PayPal’s website. PayPal processes the payment under its own privacy information and terms. Card details are not entered into this website. A merchant payment record can include the payer’s identity, amount and transaction details needed to reconcile the payment.

Your information

We do not sell the information you send us. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or private customer records through an inquiry. Ask about secure access separately if a project requires it.

To ask what information we hold, correct a mistake or request deletion, email hello@knoxsitefactory.com. We will explain any business records we need to retain.
