Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE — set when published]

Knox Site Factory is a small web-design service in the Knoxville, Tennessee area, run by Josh Stone as a sole proprietor. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms (quote request, site review, intake): for example your name, email, phone, business name, website address and message.
  • Payment details: we do not collect them. If you pay the deposit, you do it on PayPal's own page. PayPal tells us who paid and how much; we never see your card number.
  • Emails you send to hello@knoxsitefactory.com.
  • Calls, texts and voicemails to our business number, 865-551-9084.
  • Site analytics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see overall visit numbers, such as which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from, and how fast pages load. It reports totals, not individual people. It sets no cookies and does not track you across other websites.

How we use it

To answer your request, send a quote or proposal, build and support your website, take payment, and keep business records. We do not sell your information.

Who handles it for us

  • Form handling: Google. Our forms send your answers to a Google Apps Script in our Google Workspace account, which saves them in a Google Sheet and emails them to us.
  • Payments: PayPal.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages.
  • Email: forwarded by our domain provider to our inbox.
  • Phone and texts: Google Voice.
  • Site analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Those services have their own privacy policies. We share your information with others only if the law requires it or you ask us to.

How long we keep it

If you contact us or fill in a form and don’t become a client, we keep your information for 24 months and then delete it. If you become a client, we keep your records as long as we need them to do the work and to meet tax and legal requirements.

Your choices

Email hello@knoxsitefactory.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. We aim to reply within one business day.

Cookies

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies. Our analytics tool, Cloudflare Web Analytics, works without cookies. PayPal’s own pages may set their own cookies when you pay there; see PayPal’s privacy policy.

Children

This service is for businesses, not children.
