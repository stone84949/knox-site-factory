How website projects work

Use these details to decide whether a focused website project fits your business. A written proposal and project agreement establish the scope, price, timing and payment terms before work starts.

Website Launch: $1,500

Five purposeful pages: Home, Services, About, Service Area, and Contact/Quote. The package includes mobile-first design, plain-language copy editing, call and quote paths, a technical SEO foundation, accessibility and performance review, and two revision rounds.

The standard payment split is $750 to schedule and $750 after approval, before launch. Use the website’s $750 PayPal link only after signing your project agreement and confirming that standard deposit amount.

Founding-client offer: $1,000

The first three suitable clients may receive the launch for $1,000 with separate written permission for a truthful case study after delivery. The discount does not depend on a positive testimonial. The founding payment split is $500 to schedule and $500 before launch. After signing your project agreement, use your separate $500 invoice; do not use the standard $750 deposit link.

Optional Care Plan: $99 per month

Care includes managed hosting, SSL, backups, monitoring, updates, and up to 30 minutes of small content edits per month, plus a quarterly website-health and conversion review. It is month to month. It is a separate choice from the website launch.

Ownership and boundaries

You retain ownership of your domain, content and business accounts. A written scope records the deliverables, access and handoff. Paid advertising, guaranteed rankings or leads, custom software, e-commerce, unlimited revisions and ongoing content production are outside the focused launch unless separately agreed.

Before a deposit

Confirm the authorized decision-maker, scope, schedule assumptions, revisions, third-party fees, ownership, payment split and refund/cancellation terms in writing. No project-specific refund rule is established by this information page. If you have not received your project agreement, ask Josh for it before paying.
