Questions people ask before they call

Still not sure? Call 865-555-0107. Asking is free.

Is a flickering light dangerous?

One flickering bulb is usually the bulb or fixture. Flickering across the house, or lights that dim hard when appliances start, can point to a loose connection and should be checked soon.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. Many homes with a 200-amp panel can add a charger. We check your panel and usage first and only suggest an upgrade if it’s needed.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, when the job calls for one, like a panel change or new circuit. The permit cost is listed in your written price.

Do you charge to come out?

Yes. A service fee covers the trip and troubleshooting. We tell you the amount on the phone before we book.
