How a lead moves through

Each homeowner inquiry follows the same path. The client controls the job; the portal records where each one stands.

1 · SourcedAn inquiry comes in through the client's site, phone line or local search.
2 · QualifiedKSF checks it's a homeowner in the service area with a real need and a verified number.
3 · BookedThe appointment lands on the client's calendar with day, time and job notes.
4 · ClosedThe client marks the outcome. Some close, some don't — no volume is promised.
