Repairs

Most calls are one of four parts. Here's where they live on your door.

ASprings

Torsion springs above the door do the lifting. We replace them in pairs so they wear evenly.

BCables

A frayed or loose cable makes a door hang crooked. Stop using it and call.

CRollers

Worn rollers are the usual cause of a loud, rumbling door.

DTracks

Bent or loose tracks get straightened and tightened, or replaced.

    How long do springs last?

    Most standard springs are rated for about 10,000 open-and-close cycles. For a family that uses the door four times a day, that's roughly seven years. Heavier use wears them out sooner. If one spring breaks, the other usually isn't far behind, so we replace both.

    Do you charge to come out?

    Yes, there's a trip fee, and we tell you what it is on the phone before we schedule anything. No surprises at the door. You get a price for the repair before we start, and you can say no.

    Can you match my neighbors' doors?

    Usually, yes. Send us a photo or the brand and model if your HOA has one on file. We'll find the same door or the closest match and bring a sample so you can compare it in daylight.

    How long does a new door take?

    It depends on the door and the maker. Some stock doors arrive quickly, while custom colors and windows take longer. We confirm the timing when you order. Installing is usually done in a single visit.

    Is it safe to use the door if it's making noise?

    A little squeak is normal wear. A grinding sound, a jerking door, or a door that stops halfway is a reason to stop using it and call. Those can mean a worn roller, a cable problem, or a spring that's about to go.
