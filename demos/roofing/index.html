How a roof visit works

Same order, every job. No surprises on the invoice.

  1. You call or send photos

    Tell us what you see inside and out. Photos from the ground are plenty.

  2. We look, with photos

    We go up, take pictures of the problem areas, and check the attic if we can get to it.

  3. You get it in writing

    A written estimate with photos, the repair option and, if it makes sense, the replacement option.

  4. We do the work and clean up

    Tarps on the landscaping, a magnet sweep for nails, and photos of the finished job.

Questions people ask before they call

Still not sure? Call 865-555-0105. Asking is free.

Will you just patch it, or do I need a new roof?

We’ll give you both answers when both are honest options. Lots of leaks come from one flashing, vent boot or a few shingles, and a repair is the right call.

Do you work with insurance?

We’ll document storm damage with photos and a written report, and meet the adjuster if you want. Your policy and the adjuster decide what’s covered.

How long does a replacement take?

Most houses take one or two days, depending on size, steepness and weather. We tarp anything that’s open overnight.

Do I need to be home?

Not for the outside work. We’d like to check the attic, so we’ll set a time for that part if you want it included.
