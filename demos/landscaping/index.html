Questions people ask before they call

Still not sure? Call 865-555-0106. Asking is free.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Regular mowing is month to month. We ask for a week’s notice if you want to stop.

What if it rains on my day?

We move you to the next dry day and text you the new day. We don’t mow soaked lawns because it tears up the grass.

Do you haul away the clippings and leaves?

Leaves and cleanup debris are hauled away. Grass clippings are mulched back into the lawn unless you ask us to bag them.

Can you plant in our clay soil?

Yes. We amend the bed and pick plants that handle East Tennessee clay, then tell you how often to water them the first summer.
