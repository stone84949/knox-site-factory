Questions people ask before they call

Still not sure? Call 865-555-0104. Asking is free.

Do you charge to come out?

Yes. A trip fee covers the drive and finding the problem. We tell you the amount on the phone before we book, so it’s never a surprise.

Do you really answer at night?

Yes, for real emergencies like burst pipes or sewage backing up. After-hours visits cost more, and we tell you how much before anyone drives out.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If it’s over about 10 years old and leaking from the tank, replacement usually makes sense. Most other problems, like a bad thermostat or element, are repairs.

Where is my main water shutoff?

Usually where the water line enters the house: in a basement, crawlspace, garage or near the water heater. There’s also one at the meter by the street. We’ll show you on our first visit.
