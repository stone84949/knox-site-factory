# Website self-check content

Audience: Knoxville-area home-service business owners evaluating their existing website.

## Contents

- [Owner-facing checklist](website-self-check.md): practical observations, one chosen fix, and a retest record.
- [Companion content](website-self-check-companions.md): short article, three social captions, and an optional introduction for the site-review page.
- [HTML checklist](../website-self-check/index.html) and [article](../website-self-check/before-a-redesign.html).

These resources support the existing sales page, demos and site-review page without adding another offer or dependency.

## Inquiry behavior

The homepage and site-review page use an email-app link and render no lead form. Opening the draft does not send a message or confirm inbox receipt. The sample-site interactions remain labeled demonstrations. See [form asset notes](../forms/README.md) for the dormant submission assets and the separate review and end-to-end inbox test required to restore a form.

## Review scope

Check local links, all five checklist sections, the three captions, keyboard controls and print layout. Review copy for fabricated proof, outdated offers and false submission claims. Preview and noindex restrictions remain in place; this content does not establish public release or operational email delivery.
