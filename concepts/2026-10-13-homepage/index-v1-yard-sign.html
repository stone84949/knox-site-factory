Plain answers

Who owns the site?

You own the domain, the approved content, the analytics, and your business accounts. Some third-party media, like licensed stock photos, may stay licensed rather than owned.

Is ongoing SEO included?

No. The launch includes a technical foundation: crawlable pages, titles and descriptions, business data, a sitemap, and indexability checks. Nobody here will promise you a ranking.

What does the care plan cover?

Hosting, backups, monitoring, security updates, up to 30 minutes of small edits a month, and a quarterly health review. New pages, campaigns, photography, and large revisions are separate work.

Are the demo sites real businesses?

No. All three demos are labeled concepts. They carry no real testimonials, ratings, licenses, customer counts, or results.
